Option 01

On-Court Training Sessions

A team training session built around what your players need. Book a one-off visit or bring me in occasionally when a team needs extra support.

  • Shooting, finishing, ball handling, footwork, passing and defence.
  • Spacing, moving without the ball, help defence and rotations.
  • Offensive plays, team structure and understanding roles.
  • Reading the defence, making decisions and applying skills in games.

I speak with the coach beforehand to agree on the focus. We break skills and concepts down, then use realistic activities that give players decisions to make. Your coach stays involved so they understand the teaching points and can reinforce them afterwards.

Option 02

Eight-Week Team Development Program

A structured block for teams that want to build on the work over time. We agree on a couple of development goals, then progress the sessions together with the existing coach.

  • Four on-court sessions per team, delivered fortnightly over eight weeks.
  • Each visit fits the team’s usual 45–60-minute training slot.
  • An initial coach conversation to understand ability, needs and priorities.
  • Four simple reinforcement plans per team for the alternate weeks, with drills, teaching points and adjustments.
  • Brief feedback during visits and a final progress discussion.

Priorities might include defensive pressure, help defence and rotations, transition offence, offensive spacing, player movement or creating better shots. Each session builds on the previous work.

The reinforcement plans are practical guides adapted to your team’s focus. Your coach continues running the team and coaching games.

Option 03

Club Coaches Workshops

A 60-minute sit-down workshop for your club’s coaches, especially parent and volunteer coaches who want practical support and a clearer training structure.

  • Structure a 45-minute session and balance teaching, practice and games.
  • Prioritise skills and team concepts for different ages.
  • Choose useful activities and keep players active and involved.
  • Coach different ability levels and adjust drills that are too easy or too hard.
  • Know what to look for when teaching a skill and give clear feedback.

Coaches can bring questions and examples from their own teams. We work through situations they’re actually facing, so the ideas are useful at their next training.

Option 05

Mental-Performance Workshops

Practical basketball mental-performance education for your club’s players and teams. Give athletes ways to work on the mental side alongside their on-court training.

I created Peak State Playbook after struggling with confidence, overthinking, pressure and hesitating on open shots. Changing my approach to mental training helped me go from around 4 to 24 points per game in one season. That’s my personal experience, rather than a promised result for participants.

  • Build confidence and handle pressure.
  • Respond to mistakes and stay locked in.
  • Prepare mentally for games.
  • Manage overthinking and hesitation.
  • Develop stronger performance habits.

The sessions use basketball situations and practical tools players can take into training and games. Coach or parent sessions can also be discussed.
