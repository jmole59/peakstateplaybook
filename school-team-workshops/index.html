Program Formats

Choose your workshop format

01 / Introduction

Single Workshop

60–90 minutes

One interactive workshop during school hours, team training or a development session.

Delivered in one 60–90 minute session, with basketball-specific discussion and activities that introduce practical mental-performance tools athletes can use in games.

Best for a team or school wanting a focused starting point.

02 / Deeper Dive

Extended Workshop

2–4 hours

A longer session to explore and practise a wider range of mental-performance skills.

Delivered as a 2–4 hour intensive during a scheduled development block. More time for basketball scenarios, discussion and active practice means athletes can explore the tools in greater depth and leave with clearer ways to apply them.

Best for a group ready to dedicate a longer session to the mental side of performance.

03 / Ongoing Development

Multi-Session Program

2–4 weeks

One workshop each week to build understanding and apply the tools over time.

Across 2–4 weeks, Jarrod comes in once a week during a school period or team training session. Each workshop builds on the last, giving athletes time to practise key tools between visits and bring what they learn back into training and games.

Best for schools and teams wanting consistent development across several weeks.
