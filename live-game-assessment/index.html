FROM OBSERVATION TO ACTION

FEEDBACK THEY CAN
TAKE INTO THE NEXT GAME

Three priorities, selected for the athlete. Here’s what that can look like.

01

Use the gap

OBSERVATION
“You had room to drive four times but moved the ball on without attacking.”
COACHING FEEDBACK
The gap was there. By the time you decided, the defender had recovered.
NEXT-GAME PRIORITY
When the defender closes out, check the space and the help. If the lane is open, go. You don’t need to know you’ll score to make a good decision.
02

Keep track of your player

OBSERVATION
“Twice, your player cut behind you while you were watching the ball.”
COACHING FEEDBACK
You were ready to help, but you lost sight of your own player.
NEXT-GAME PRIORITY
Open up your stance so you can see the ball and your player. Check them again when the ball moves, before they start the cut.
03

Stay in the next play

OBSERVATION
“After the turnover, you stopped moving and passed up an open shot on the next possession.”
COACHING FEEDBACK
One mistake changed how you played the next two possessions. You still had a job to do.
NEXT-GAME PRIORITY
After a mistake, get back and do the next job. If the next shot is a good one, take it. The turnover doesn’t change whether you’re open.

Illustrative coaching examples only. Your athlete’s priorities come from their own game.

A FEW PRACTICAL DETAILS

BEFORE YOU ENQUIRE

When will we receive the report?

Your report delivery date will be agreed before payment.

Does my child need to be a representative player?

No. Domestic and representative players can benefit. The assessment takes account of age, position, competition level and role.

Is this a training session or an in-person game assessment?

It is an in-person game observation followed by a written report. The standalone assessment does not include an on-court training session. The A$499 bundle adds three one-on-one Film Room video reviews of approximately 60 minutes each.

Do we pay before the game is confirmed?

No. Enquire first. Jmole checks the venue, date and availability, confirms the service and delivery timing, then sends a payment link. Attendance is scheduled after payment.

Will my child know you are attending?

Attendance is arranged with appropriate parent or guardian consent and venue, club and safeguarding requirements. A low-key sideline presence can help the athlete play naturally; this is not covert monitoring. Photos or recordings require separate permission.

What if there is limited playing time?

The report reflects what could be observed. Where there is not enough evidence, a category is marked Not Observed. Scores are not lowered simply because an opportunity did not arise.

Does an existing Film Room Pro observation include this report?

Not automatically. Existing package entitlements stay as described in that package. This full written assessment is a separate service.
