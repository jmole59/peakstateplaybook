Offence

03

Reads, situational awareness and the adjustments you make as the game changes.

Decisiveness, visible responses to mistakes and sustained engagement.

06

Leadership & communication

Communication, teamwork, support for teammates and responses to coaching.

Quietness is not a low confidence score, and leadership is not simply being loud. We assess observable actions and contribution, without diagnosing emotions or psychological conditions.

An illustrative look inside

MORE THAN
JUST A BOX SCORE.

Every assessment looks beyond points and statistics to evaluate the decisions, habits and details that impact performance.

Detailed game observations help identify patterns, missed opportunities and areas where small adjustments can make a big difference.

Jmole generally selects four or five relevant observations, such as good shots taken, scoring opportunities passed up, defensive recoveries or effective cuts. This is not a comprehensive official statistics service.

Ratings describe the observed game, not a permanent measure of ability or future potential. Any category without enough evidence is marked Not Observed and excluded from the overall average.

How to read the ratings

9–10: excellent decisions and execution · 7–8: strong performance · 5–6: inconsistent, with missed opportunities · 3–4: significant development opportunities · 1–2: very limited demonstration. All ratings are interpreted in the athlete’s playing context.

Before you enquire

PARENT QUESTIONS

What is a Live Game Assessment?

Coach Jmole attends one basketball game and observes the athlete in context. You receive a structured four-to-five-page report and three personalised development priorities.

Do you only look at points and statistics?

No. We look at decisions, defence, off-ball movement, basketball IQ, communication and contribution. A strong scoring night and a strong all-round performance are not always the same thing.

How is the athlete rated?

Six categories receive one rating out of 10 where there is enough evidence. The overall Game Performance Rating is the average of the categories that can fairly be assessed. A category with insufficient evidence is marked Not Observed and excluded from that average.

Will my child know you are attending?

The observation can be arranged discreetly to help the athlete play naturally, with appropriate parent or guardian consent and subject to venue and club requirements. This is not covert monitoring. Photographs or recordings require separate permission.

Is this suitable for domestic and representative players?

Yes. The assessment takes account of the athlete’s age, competition level, position, role and playing opportunities. It reflects this particular game, rather than rating their future potential.

Where are assessments available?

At selected Melbourne basketball venues within approximately 45 minutes of Boronia. All bookings are subject to game location and availability. Enquiries outside the standard area are considered individually before any booking is accepted.

How many assessments are available each week?

A maximum of four athletes per week. Friday evenings and Saturday mornings are the usual capacity windows, with actual availability depending on game times, venues, travel and scheduling.

What is included in the $499 bundle?

The complete Live Game Assessment and written report, plus three personalised one-on-one Film Room video calls of approximately 60 minutes each. Submitted game footage lets us review progress against the development priorities. The calls are arranged separately.

Do I pay before the game is confirmed?

No. Enquire first. Jmole checks the venue, game time and availability, then confirms the details and provides a payment link. Your assessment is scheduled after payment. Submitting this form does not confirm a booking.

Is the observation in Complete Season Film Room Pro the same service?

Not automatically. The existing package’s eligible in-person observation remains as described in that package. The full written assessment report is part of this separate service and is not automatically added to existing package entitlements.

What if there is not enough playing time to assess something?

The report reflects what could actually be observed. Limited opportunities are taken into account, and a category is marked Not Observed where there is insufficient evidence. Scores are not lowered simply because an opportunity did not arise.

When will the report arrive?

The delivery timeframe will be confirmed with you before payment, along with the accepted game details and next steps.
