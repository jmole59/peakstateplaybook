Privacy Policy

How Peak State Playbook handles your personal information.
Peak State Playbook · ABN 38 498 402 171
Last updated: 6 October 2026

Information you provide

We collect details you choose to provide through enquiries, lesson requests, program registrations and athlete forms. These may include your name, email address and information relevant to the service you request.

Your free lesson request

To email you the exclusive G.A.M.E. Plan lesson, we need your email address. Your first name is optional. We also record your request time, request identifier, optional marketing choice and email delivery status. We use these records to fulfil your request, handle delivery problems, prevent duplicate emails and protect the signup from abuse. Without an email address, we cannot send the lesson.

Your contact and request records are stored in our private Athlete Hub. They are separate from athlete baseline responses. If you have already submitted a baseline using the same email address, we may link the records internally to manage your athlete relationship. A lesson request does not create a Circle account, enrol you in the paid program or indicate that you have purchased it.

Optional marketing

Requesting the lesson does not subscribe you to promotional emails. The separate marketing checkbox is optional and unchecked by default. If you choose it, we record your permission to send occasional basketball mental-performance tips and program updates. You can withdraw permission using an unsubscribe option in a marketing email or by emailing us. You can still receive the requested lesson without choosing marketing.

Services used to deliver the lesson

  • Netlify hosts the website and processes the signup through a server function.
  • Our private Athlete Hub stores contact, request, consent and delivery records using Cloudflare Workers and D1, with hosting and owner access provided through ChatGPT Sites.
  • Resend processes your email address, optional name and lesson email to send it and report delivery, bounce or complaint events.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks the signup for spam and abuse. It processes technical browser and connection information for that check.
  • The lesson link opens YouTube. YouTube handles viewing information under its own privacy policy.

These providers process information needed to operate their services outside Australia as well as through their global infrastructure. We have not confirmed a specific country for the Hub’s D1 database or this website’s Netlify function region, and do not claim Australian-only storage. Resend sends the lesson through its configured Tokyo region (ap-northeast-1), but stores customer data in the United States; sending location and storage location are different. This is not a complete list of processing countries. Their privacy information is available from Netlify, Cloudflare, OpenAI, Resend and Google / YouTube.

Other program services

Program access and purchases take place through Circle and its payment services. Athlete baseline forms use Tally and our private Athlete Hub. These services have their own privacy practices. Requesting a free lesson does not automatically add you to Circle or submit an athlete baseline.

Storage and access

Contact records are held in the private Hub rather than displayed publicly on this website. Access is restricted through the Hub’s owner authentication. Contact records, signup history, consent records and email delivery events currently have no automatic expiry or deletion schedule. A defined retention period and lead-deletion workflow are not yet implemented. Unsubscribing changes your marketing status but does not erase your record; bounce and complaint suppression also retains the contact. Deleting a Tally assessment does not delete a free-lesson contact. Provider logs and backups have separate retention arrangements. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion. We will assess and respond to deletion requests individually, including any records needed to honour an unsubscribe or suppression request or meet legal obligations; we do not promise immediate or automatic deletion.

Questions and privacy requests

Email info@peakstateplaybook.com for access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of marketing permission or a privacy complaint. We may ask for information to confirm your identity before releasing or changing a record. We will review your request and respond with the next steps.
