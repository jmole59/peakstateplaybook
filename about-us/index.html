Why I Built Peak State

I knew my game was there. I just couldn't always access it.

There were stretches where confidence never seemed to match ability. One mistake could turn into overthinking. One missed shot could change how the next possession felt.

That experience pushed me to learn how to train the mental side of basketball with the same intent I brought to skill work. Peak State Playbook grew from that process: practical systems athletes can actually use when pressure, mistakes and doubt show up.

The goal isn't to feel perfect. It's to have a system you can trust when the game gets hard.

The Approach

Systems over hype.

  • Train confidence instead of waiting for it
  • Build responses for mistakes and adversity
  • Use routines that make game-day preparation repeatable
  • Develop standards that hold when emotion changes
  • Make mental performance practical and basketball-specific
