Connect training to competition

MORE TRAINING ISN’T
ALWAYS THE ANSWER

You might have the skill. The question is when and how you use it. Another shooting workout will not show you the open teammate you missed or why you rushed a good advantage.

Film connects what you train to what actually happens in games. We can pause, replay and look at the options you had, then choose a practical adjustment.

  • Controlling pace
  • Shot selection
  • Spacing and positioning
  • Recognising advantages
  • Defensive positioning and rotations
Your questions, answered

Film Room FAQs.

What is a Film Room review?

A personalised live one-on-one video-call session where you and Jmole analyse your actual game footage, discuss your decisions and identify actionable development priorities. The free three-clip offer is separate recorded feedback.

How long does each review take?

Road to Rep reviews are approximately 45 minutes. Single Film Room, the 5-Session Film Room Pack, Representative Season and Complete Season Pro reviews are approximately 60 minutes.

What makes Complete Season Pro different?

Pro includes 25 purchased reviews across eligible representative grading and championship games, FREE additional reviews of eligible official finals if your team qualifies, and one in-person observation in metropolitan Melbourne. Representative Season includes 18 reviews, with eligible finals using any remaining purchased reviews.

Can I choose my games and review frequency?

Yes, within your package’s eligibility and review allowance. Road to Rep and Representative Season may be used weekly or fortnightly. Weekly reviews are recommended for consistent development, especially with Complete Season Pro. Not every game is automatically reviewed.

More questions
Can Road to Rep be used for representative games?

No. Road to Rep is for domestic basketball games only, during one nominated domestic season.

Can representative packages be used for domestic games?

No. Representative Season Film Room and Complete Season Film Room Pro cover eligible representative basketball games only.

Can I use Representative Season during grading and finals?

Yes. Allocate the 18 included reviews across eligible representative grading, championship and official finals games. There is no fixed allocation. Finals are not complimentary and require a remaining purchased review.

What happens if my team does not make finals?

The complimentary finals benefit in Complete Season Pro is not used. It cannot be exchanged for cash or replacement complimentary reviews. Finals qualification is not guaranteed.

Is the in-person observation available Australia-wide?

No. Complete Season Pro includes one observation in metropolitan Melbourne, subject to availability and mutual agreement. It is separate from the 25 reviews. Customers outside metropolitan Melbourne must contact Jmole before purchasing; no interstate visit or replacement benefit is promised.

Can I use season reviews across multiple seasons, or request an injury extension?

The three season packages are intended for one nominated competition season. The final unused-review policy and significant injury or illness extension process are still being finalised. Contact Jmole before enrolling to confirm the applicable arrangements. No automatic forfeiture, indefinite carryover or full-package renewal is promised.

Are practice games and tournaments eligible?

Practice-game and tournament eligibility is not yet finalised. Ask Jmole to confirm the specific competition before relying on it as an eligible game.

Can I try Film Room first?

Yes. The Free 3-Clip Game Review provides short personalised recorded feedback once per athlete. It does not automatically enrol you in a paid package.
