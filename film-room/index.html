Connect training to competition

ALWAYS THE ANSWER

Film connects what you train to what actually happens in games. We can pause, replay and look at the options you had, then choose a practical adjustment.

Your questions, answered

Film Room FAQs.

Which package is right for me?

Choose a Single Session for one game or a 5-Session Pack for flexibility. Road to Rep is focused development for domestic players. The VJBL packages support championship competition or the full season including grading.

How long is each review?

Road to Rep reviews are approximately 45 minutes. All other paid options are approximately 60 minutes. The free review is short recorded feedback on three clips.

Does Road to Rep cover Summer and Winter?

One purchase covers one nominated domestic season. Summer and Winter are separate purchases. Athlete-selected eligible games use the ten purchased credits; not every game is automatically reviewed.

Are finals reviews included?

Championship athletes can use remaining purchased credits on eligible finals, with one credit used per completed review. Full Season + Grading includes complimentary eligible official VJBL finals reviews for games actually played by the athlete. No fixed number or finals qualification is promised. Road to Rep has no additional complimentary finals reviews.

What does the game observation include?

Full Season + Grading includes one in-person observation at an eligible metropolitan Melbourne game, subject to mutually agreed scheduling, reasonable travel and availability. It is not an extra Film Room review; a dedicated review may use a purchased credit.

Are there limits on season enrolments?

A maximum of ten athletes is shared across both VJBL season packages. If places fill, contact Jmole about the waitlist. Road to Rep availability is confirmed separately. No unverified remaining-place count is displayed.

What if I join late, or need payment or booking terms?

Enquire before joining a season package to confirm eligible games, credit expiry, submission deadlines, cancellation and rescheduling terms. Instalment arrangements and observation scheduling fallbacks are still being finalised. No mid-season discount is assumed.

Can I try it first?

Yes. The Free 3-Clip Game Review is available once per athlete through the existing form. Duplicate requests do not start another free review. Single and five-session options are available for further feedback.
