Connect training to competition

MORE TRAINING ISN’T
ALWAYS THE ANSWER

You might have the skill. The question is when and how you use it. Another shooting workout will not show you the open teammate you missed or why you rushed a good advantage.

Film connects what you train to what actually happens in games. We can pause, replay and look at the options you had, then choose a practical adjustment.

  • Reading the defence
  • Controlling pace
  • Shot selection
  • Spacing and positioning
  • Recognising advantages
  • Responding after mistakes
