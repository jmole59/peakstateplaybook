Why Film

Training builds skill. Film builds awareness.

Film can expose habits, positioning, missed reads and decision patterns that are difficult to see in the moment. The goal is to make your training more specific and your next steps clearer.

  • Decision-making under pressure
  • Shot selection and scoring reads
  • Defensive positioning and awareness
  • Movement, spacing and off-ball habits
  • Clear tactical adjustments
