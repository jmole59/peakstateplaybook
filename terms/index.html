Film Room

Film Room package terms

Paid Film Room reviews are personalised one-on-one live video calls with Jmole. Single Film Room and the 5-Session Film Room Pack reviews are approximately 60 minutes. Road to Rep reviews are approximately 45 minutes; Representative Season Film Room and Complete Season Film Room Pro reviews are approximately 60 minutes. Scheduling is arranged directly with Jmole.

Eligible games and season packages

Road to Rep includes 10 reviews of domestic games within one nominated season. Representative Season Film Room includes 18 reviews of representative games, allocated flexibly across eligible grading, championship and official finals games in one nominated season. Finals reviews use the remaining purchased allowance in that package. Complete Season Film Room Pro includes 25 purchased reviews of eligible representative grading and championship games within one nominated season. Weekly or fortnightly reviews can be arranged. No package guarantees representative selection or a particular performance result.

Complete Season Film Room Pro bonuses

Pro includes additional free reviews of eligible official finals games actually played by the athlete’s team in the nominated season, where the team qualifies. These do not reduce the 25 purchased reviews. No fixed number of finals reviews is promised. If the team does not qualify or play finals, this bonus has no replacement or exchange value.

Pro also includes one in-person game observation in metropolitan Melbourne, subject to mutually agreed scheduling and availability. This is additional to the live video-call reviews and does not count as one of the 25 reviews. Interstate athletes should contact Jmole before purchasing to discuss whether the package is suitable; no alternative observation benefit or price adjustment is currently promised.
