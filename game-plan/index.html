The Story Behind The System

WHY I BUILT THE G.A.M.E. PLAN

For years, I knew I was capable of more, but my confidence never seemed to match my ability. I would miss one shot, start overthinking, and suddenly feel like I was playing a completely different game.

Eventually, I started learning how to train the mental side of basketball the same way I trained my skills. Those lessons helped me stop fearing mistakes, recover faster and play with more freedom.

The Four Areas

BUILT FROM THE INSIDE OUT

The G.A.M.E. Plan focuses on four key areas that shape how you think, respond and perform when the game gets tough.
01
Identity
Build confidence around who you are as a player, rather than allowing every make, miss or result to define you.
02
State
Learn to manage your body, emotions and focus so you can stay composed and ready when pressure starts to rise.
03
Response
Develop better responses to mistakes, bad calls, missed shots and adversity so one moment doesn't control the next.
04
Standards
Build the habits, behaviours and expectations that keep your approach consistent regardless of the result.
More Than Information

A COMPLETE SYSTEM FOR REAL GROWTH

You won't just learn concepts. You'll work through a structured process designed to help you understand them, apply them and use them in real game situations.
01
Understand
Recognise the thoughts, patterns and reactions that currently shape the way you perform.
02
Build
Develop practical mental tools that give you a better way to approach confidence, pressure and adversity.
03
Apply
Take those tools into training and game situations so they become something you can actually use under pressure.
04
Reinforce
Keep reviewing, adjusting and strengthening the behaviours that help you compete with more consistency.
The goal isn't to feel confident every second. It's to build a system that helps you keep playing your game when confidence is tested.
