Built from playing and coaching

Why I built
this system

I knew my ability was there. But one missed shot could turn into overthinking, hesitation and a completely different game.

Learning to train identity, confidence, preparation and response changed how I could access the skills I had already worked on. Peak State Playbook grew from that process, with practical systems players can use when pressure and doubt show up.

Created by Jarrod Molnar (Coach Jmole), with professional playing experience across Australia, China, Taiwan and Malaysia and 15 years of coaching and private training.

From joining to using it in games

What actually happens
after you join

Watch the teaching. Work through your own game. Practice the tools on court. This is what the athlete does, with a parent welcome to support the process.

  1. 01

    Start with your baseline

    After purchase, access the program, Vault and community. Complete the Athlete Baseline Form to establish your starting point before entering the program. Your physical workbook is sent to you; a digital version is also included.

  2. 02

    Follow the four levels

    Move through Identity, State, Response and Standards in order. Each level builds another part of the system, from the player you choose to be to the standards you practice.

  3. 03

    Watch, write, apply

    Follow the workbook and watch the matching video stages. Pause to complete your own reflections and exercises. Some workbook pages provide context or set standards rather than adding another video.

  4. 04

    Return to your Vault

    Use the Peak State Vault to find tools, worksheets and guided audios without replaying the whole course. Return to the resource that fits the situation you are working on.

  5. 05

    Take it onto the court

    Use your Game-Day Routine before games, practice the Bounce Back Blueprint after difficult moments, and reflect with the Post-Game System. The aim is to use the tools around real training and games.

Your questions, answered

Before you begin

What is The G.A.M.E. Plan, and who is it for?

A complete basketball mental-performance system for players who want to develop confidence, composure and more consistent responses under pressure. Parents can use this page to understand the structured education, workbook and support their athlete receives.

How long does it take, and when do I get access?

You get immediate access to the full G.A.M.E. Plan, Vault and community after purchase. Work through the lessons and workbook, then practice the tools around training and games. Allow time to apply the tools between lessons rather than rushing through the videos.

What is included in the core program?

50 video lessons, the physical and digital workbook, Peak State Vault, guided audios, practical mental-performance tools, and access to the members’ community and support.

What is the Peak State Vault?

A quick-access library of tools, worksheets and guided audios, so you can return to what you need without searching through the whole program.

More questions +
Is this designed for my child to complete independently? Can I support them?

The lessons speak directly to the athlete and guide them through their own workbook. A parent can watch alongside them and discuss what they are learning, while the athlete writes their own answers and practices the tools.

What age or level is it best suited for?

The program focuses on basketball situations such as pressure, mistakes, preparation and response. If you are unsure whether the material suits your child’s age or current level, email info@peakstateplaybook.com before purchasing for guidance.

How much time will they need each week?

Work progressively through the workbook and matching video stages, leaving time to apply the tools at training and games. A fixed completion schedule, weekly time commitment and individual lesson lengths are not specified here. For guidance, contact info@peakstateplaybook.com before purchasing.

What if my child is already confident, or confidence is not the only issue?

Confidence is one part of the system. The four levels also address identity, preparation, body, focus, language, responses to adversity and consistent standards. An athlete can work on these areas even when they already feel confident.

Is this therapy or basketball skills coaching?

The core program teaches basketball mental-performance tools through lessons and workbook activities. It is not a therapy or psychology service. It focuses on how athletes prepare and respond, rather than on-court skills training.

What happens immediately after purchase?

You receive access to the full program, Peak State Vault and members’ community. Begin with the Athlete Baseline Form, then follow the workbook sequence and matching videos. A physical workbook is sent to you, with digital workbook access also included.

The Vault is designed as a resource to return to when applying the system. A specific membership access duration is not stated here. Confirm the duration with info@peakstateplaybook.com before joining.

Is the workbook physical, digital or both?

Both. A physical workbook is sent to you and a digital version is included. They support the video lessons with your own written reflections, routines and standards; they are part of the program rather than a separate optional extra.
