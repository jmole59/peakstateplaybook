When ability meets pressure

You know your game.
But can you trust it?

You train with confidence, then hold back once the game starts.

One missed shot changes how aggressive you are.

You hesitate on shots you normally take.

A turnover or bad call stays with you into the next possession.

You worry about being benched or what your coach thinks.

You judge your whole performance by your points.

You train your shot, handle, body and conditioning. The mental side deserves that same structure. The G.A.M.E. Plan helps you build a repeatable system for training, game day and the moments that test you.

Your questions, answered

Before you begin

What is The G.A.M.E. Plan, and who is it for?

A complete basketball mental-performance system for players who want to develop confidence, composure and more consistent responses under pressure. Parents can use this page to understand the structured education, workbook and support their athlete receives.

How long does it take, and when do I get access?

Program access begins on 1 November 2026. Work through the lessons and workbook, then practise the tools around training and games. Allow time to apply the tools between lessons rather than rushing through the videos.

What is included in the core program?

The complete video curriculum, physical and digital workbook, Peak State Vault, guided audios, practical mental-performance tools, and access to the members’ community and support.

What is the Peak State Vault?

A quick-access library of tools, worksheets and guided audios, so you can return to what you need without searching through the whole program.

What are the Founding Athlete bonuses?

Purchases before 1 November include three flexible private coaching credits and a separate 5-Point Performance Plan call. The package has up to A$549 additional value and is included at no additional cost.

How do the three coaching credits work?

Choose any combination of Mental Performance Sessions, Game Film Review & Performance Analysis, or eligible Melbourne on-court sessions. You can choose three mental-performance sessions, three film reviews, one of each or another combination. Credits are valid for 12 months from purchase, for personal use, non-transferable, have no cash value and are subject to scheduling availability.

What if I live outside Melbourne?

Choose mental-performance coaching or film review by private video call. On-court training is Melbourne only, subject to reasonable location, court and scheduling availability. An on-court session can be exchanged for either video-call option.

What is the 5-Point Performance Plan?

A separate private 30-minute call with Coach J-Mo to build next-step priorities across mental performance, basketball development, physical preparation, recovery and lifestyle, and leadership and standards. It is recommended after completing the program, preferably within approximately 30 days of completion, and remains redeemable within 12 months of purchase. It cannot be exchanged for another coaching session.

Is there a payment plan?

Pay A$699 in full or split it into three payments of A$233. Both total A$699. Check the payment schedule and final details at checkout.

How does the 30-day guarantee work?

If you are not satisfied, request a full refund within 30 days of purchase by emailing info@peakstateplaybook.com with your purchase details. The period starts at purchase, not at program access. This extra promise does not limit your rights under Australian Consumer Law.

What happens after 31 October?

The Founding Athlete bonus package ends at 11:59 PM AEDT on 31 October 2026. From 1 November, The G.A.M.E. Plan remains A$699 and the founding bonuses are no longer included.
