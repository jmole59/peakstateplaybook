Prime Playbook

The G.A.M.E. Plan

A complete mental-performance system built to help basketball players develop identity, control state, respond under pressure and play to a higher standard.
This is not a motivational talk. The program is structured around four levels, checkpoints, stages, video lessons, workbook work and repeatable tools.
See The SystemGet Program Access
What You Build

Confidence you can train.

  • Build an identity that holds when the game gets tough
  • Control your state and stay locked in under pressure
  • Recognise what knocks you off and take control faster
  • Build belief that holds and play to your standard
  • Step onto the court with a repeatable game-day process
The Four Levels

Navigate your complete G.A.M.E. Plan.

1
Ground Your Identity

Build who you are underneath

Define your competitor, create your Confidence Code and build a Bounce-Back Blueprint to reset fast.

2
Activate Your State

Control body, focus & language

Train the 3-Point Peak, state control and a game-day routine that helps you step onto the court locked in.

3
Master Your Response

Reset fast when things go wrong

Recognise state breakers, build in-game responses and use post-game resets so your response becomes more automatic.

4
Elevate Your Standard

Trust your game & hold your standard

Strengthen belief, create leverage and build a standard and system you can rely on every time you compete.

How The Program Works

One system. Built in sequence.

Each level contains checkpoints, and each checkpoint contains stages. The video lessons and workbook sections are designed to be used together and completed in order.

01

Baseline

Establish your starting point before entering the program.

02

Video + Workbook

Move through each stage using the lesson and matching workbook section together.

03

The Vault

Return to key tools, templates and extra videos without searching through the entire program.
