The system you are buying

Four levels. One G.A.M.E. Plan

G
Level 1 · Identity

Ground your identity

Identity shapes what you believe you can do and how you see yourself when results change. Training it gives you a clearer reference point for the player you choose to be, instead of letting one game or someone else’s opinion define you.

A
Level 2 · State

Activate your state

Pressure can change your body, attention and internal language. Learning to recognise and influence those three areas helps you prepare with more intention and notice when you are drifting away from the state you want to play in.

M
Level 3 · Response

Master your response

You cannot control every shot, whistle or mistake. You can practise how you respond. A repeatable response gives you something useful to return to when frustration or doubt would otherwise take over your decisions.

E
Level 4 · Standards

Elevate your standard

Motivation and confidence change. Clear standards help you keep choosing your effort, body language and habits through those changes. This is where the system becomes something you practise, rather than something you only understand.
