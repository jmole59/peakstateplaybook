Why Peak State exists

You train your shot.You train your body.But what happens when the game gets messy?

  • You miss two shots and stop looking to score.
  • Your coach reacts and you start playing safe.
  • One mistake stays with you for the next five possessions.
  • You know what to do, but hesitate when the pressure hits.

That part of the game can be trained too.

I’ve lived this part of the game

I know what it feels like to have the skill
but not trust it.

Confidence, mistakes and overthinking changed how I played. At one stage, I was producing around four points a game. After deliberately training the mental side, I reached around 24 points a game that same season.

That experience became part of why Peak State exists: to make the mental side practical for players who know their ability is there, but don’t always trust it under pressure.

My experience shaped the approach. Every athlete’s development is different.
